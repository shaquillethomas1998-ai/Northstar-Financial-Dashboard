# Northstar Financial Dashboard

A Power BI coursework project by Shaquille Thomas, analyzing monthly income and recorded expenses for 2023–2024.

## Purpose

Make financial trends easier to explore through KPI cards, monthly comparisons, income sources and expense categories.

## Tools

Power BI · DAX · Excel

## Dashboard Features

- Six measures covering income, recorded expenses, surplus, surplus margin and year-over-year growth.
- Calendar table supporting comparisons across 2023 and 2024.
- Month and year filters.
- Charts showing monthly trends, income sources and expense categories.

## Key Findings

- 2024 income totaled 132,145 currency units, down 0.75% year over year.
- Recorded expenses totaled 42,490 currency units, up 1.40%.
- Recorded surplus was 89,655 currency units, with a 67.85% surplus margin.
- October 2024 had a recorded surplus of 1,623 currency units and a 29.48% surplus margin.
- Consulting was the largest 2024 income source at 36,757 currency units.
- Internet was the largest recorded expense category at 7,481 currency units.

## How to Explore

Download A4_Northstar_Financial_Dashboard.pbix and open it in Power BI Desktop. Use the month and year filters to explore the report.

The PDF report provides supporting discussion, and the PNG images offer previews without requiring Power BI.

## Data and Limitations

Built using an instructor-provided dataset. The currency is unspecified, so values are expressed in currency units (CU).

Recorded surplus means income minus recorded expenses. It should not be interpreted as audited net profit or a complete assessment of business profitability.
